# Hisaab Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

Hisaab is a personal money organizer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

Your financial records stay on your phone. Hisaab has no accounts, no servers and no analytics in this version, so we never receive your data.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter (accounts, transactions, budgets, udhaar records, group bills, goals, bills and settings) is saved in a database on your device. Group members are names (and optional phone numbers) that you type in yourself; they are not uploaded or shared with anyone.

Receipt photos you attach are copied into the app's private storage on your phone. The database is encrypted, and its key is kept in your phone's secure keystore.

If you set an app PIN, only a salted hash of it is stored, also in the secure keystore.

## What leaves your phone

Nothing is sent automatically. Data leaves your phone only when you choose to:

- **Backup or CSV export:** the app creates a file and opens your phone's share menu. You decide where it goes (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp). That file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.
- **WhatsApp reminder, group summary or PDF report:** the app opens WhatsApp or the share menu with a message you can read and edit before sending.
- **Feedback email:** if you choose to send feedback, your email app opens with a message addressed to us.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** for the reminders you turn on (daily log, bills, udhaar due dates, budget alerts, monthly summary).
- **Camera:** only when you tap "Camera" to photograph a receipt. Choosing a photo from the gallery uses Android's photo picker and needs no permission.
- **Biometrics:** only if you turn on fingerprint unlock. The fingerprint never leaves your phone's secure hardware; the app only receives "matched" or "not matched".

Hisaab does not read your SMS, contacts, location or microphone, and only sees the photos you pick yourself. The "paste bank SMS" feature only reads text you have copied yourself, at the moment you tap the button.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data permanently erases everything from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes all data. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it afterwards.

## Children

Hisaab is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If a future version adds sign-in, cloud backup, ads or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent where required.
